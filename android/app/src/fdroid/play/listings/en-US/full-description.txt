Better Maps is a free, open-source map app for Android that works offline and respects your privacy. It builds on CoMaps and OpenStreetMap and adds the everyday features people expect from a modern map app.

<b>Find places</b>
• Fast offline search for places, addresses and categories
• "Open now" filter and opening hours in the results
• Understands queries like "pharmacy Sunday" or "café open"
• Optional online search for regions you have not downloaded (opt-in)

<b>Public transport</b>
• Real connections by bus, tram and train with timetables and live delays (via Transitous, opt-in)
• Departures with real-time data on the place page
• Settings for transfer time, walking speed, accessibility and bike transport

<b>Traffic</b>
• Live traffic jams, closures and roadworks on the map and in car routing (TraFF)
• Optional, privacy-preserving contribution to congestion detection: anonymous, encrypted, with noise added on your device

<b>Offline and private</b>
• Maps are downloaded once and work without a connection
• No ads, no tracking; every online feature is off until you switch it on
